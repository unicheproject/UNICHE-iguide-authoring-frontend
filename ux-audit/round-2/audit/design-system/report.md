## Verdict
Token layer, type scale, colour/contrast, one focus ring, status vocabulary and self-hosted fonts are built as decided. On every Content screen no text is under 12 px; the 2 px primary ring held on 14 consecutive Tab stops. Three defects break the system: `size-control` does not exist (icon buttons 18×18); `AppDialog` has no boundary in forced colours; Visitor-app screens and preview shells keep the pre-redesign vocabulary.
